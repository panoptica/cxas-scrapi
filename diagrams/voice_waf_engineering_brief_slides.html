Why We Are Doing This Whole Thing

"The foundational premise for engineering is that we should stop presenting GECX patterns as disconnected best practices. Everything we do—Composite speech-to-speech, <5ms Python callbacks instead of slow LLM supervisors, spoken tool fillers, and 5-second tool timeouts—starts at Layer 1: protecting the 1,200ms human ear ceiling on the physical phone wire."
💡 Key Architecture Talking Tip:

Emphasize the physical wire first. When engineering teams hear 'physics of the wire' and '1,200ms human threshold', they immediately understand why we don't put LLM guardrails in the audio loop.
